BowledByHer — Platform & Guide

BowledByHer is a digital canvas for IPL fans to construct, analyze, and share their ideal 2026 playing XI. No fantasy leagues, no betting, no commercial motives — just pure cricket strategy.

What You Can Do

  • 10 IPL franchises with 2026 realistic squads
  • Batting order positions (Opener → Finisher → Bowler)
  • Real IPL rules: max 4 foreign, 1 wicket-keeper
  • Impact Players (5 max) — IPL's tactical twist
  • Captain & Vice Captain selection
  • Franchise-specific Legends alongside current stars
  • Story-ready image download & social sharing

How to Use

01
Select Franchise — Click any team card (CSK, MI, RCB, etc.) to load its player pool.
02
Drag & Drop — Drag players from left panel into the 11 slots. Touch & drag works on mobile.
03
Set Batting Order — Each slot has a position. Arrange as you like.
04
Add Impact Players — Drop players into Impact section (max 5).
05
Captain & VC — Select from dropdown menus for multipliers.
06
Save & Share — Click "Save & Share" to generate image. Share on WhatsApp, Instagram, or share anywhere.
