How to Build Your Dream Team

1

Choose Your Franchise

Click on any of the 10 IPL team cards at the top. Each team has its own unique color theme, players pool, and legendary icons!

2

Build Your Playing XI

Drag & drop players from the left panel into the 11 slots on the right. You can also tap and drag on mobile devices! Each position matters - openers, middle order, finishers, and bowlers.

3

Add Impact Players

You can select up to 5 impact players who can substitute during the match. Just drag any player to the Impact Players section below the main XI.

4

Select Captain & Vice Captain

Choose your Captain and Vice Captain from the dropdown menus.

5

Save & Share

Click "SAVE & SHARE" to download your dream team image. You can download it, share on WhatsApp, Instagram or anywhere!
